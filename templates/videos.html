About Meddy tours

Meddy Tours Company Limited is a trusted and well-established tour and travel operator based in Uganda. We are dedicated to crafting unforgettable journeys that connect visitors from around the world with the true essence of East Africa. Our passionate team of local experts is committed to sharing the incredible natural beauty, vibrant cultures, and genuine hospitality found across Uganda, Rwanda, Kenya, and Tanzania.
