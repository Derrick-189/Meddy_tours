Hiking in the Virunga Mountains has always been reported to be a breathtaking experience because of the volcanic landscapes, encountering lush bamboo and alpine forests, and often spotting wildlife like mountain gorillas and unique bird species. The treks can be challenging, demanding good physical fitness, though guides are patient and encouraging.

Golden monkeys are an endangered primate species known for their striking golden-orange fur contrasted with jet-black legs and heads, inhabiting the bamboo forests of the Virunga Mountains in Central Africa. They are social, living in troops led by a dominant male and feeding primarily on bamboo shoots and leaves, though their diet also includes fruits and small invertebrates.
